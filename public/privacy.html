Privacy Policy

Shadecode Student · Effective date: October 23, 2026

This policy explains how Shadecode Student handles information when you use the app or its website. Shadecode Student is an evolving educational product. We aim to collect and use information for clear learning, account, reliability and security purposes, not to expose a learner's academic difficulties to other students.

1. Information that may be processed

  • Account information: identifiers used to sign in, such as your email address and account ID, plus profile details you choose to provide.
  • Learning activity: lessons viewed or completed, answers and attempts, assessment results, topic-level progress, confidence or self-reported understanding, study tasks, and related events needed to provide progress and recommendations.
  • Content you provide: questions, prompts, written responses, uploaded or captured work, feedback reports, and project material you choose to submit to a feature.
  • Device and service data: app preferences, local/offline state, synchronization metadata, error diagnostics and security logs that may be generated by the app or its service providers.

The exact data processed depends on which features you use. Do not submit passwords, government identifiers, financial details, or other highly sensitive information in study prompts or feedback.

2. Why we use it

Information may be used to authenticate your account; save and synchronize your study activity; provide lessons, practice, progress views and recommendations; respond to feedback; diagnose failures; protect the service; and improve product reliability. Learning indicators are estimates based on available evidence, not a definitive judgment about a learner's ability.

3. AI-assisted features

When you use an AI-assisted feature, the relevant prompt and context needed to answer it may be sent to the AI provider configured for that feature. This can include the question or learning material you submit and the response needed for the feature. Avoid including personal information that is not needed for the task.

AI processing is different from the app's deterministic progress calculations and database storage. Provider data handling, retention and model-improvement practices depend on the provider and the account/configuration in use. We do not promise that every provider has identical terms. We do not intentionally publish one learner's private learning history to other learners or treat another learner's personal mastery state as reusable shared content.

4. Service providers and sharing

The service relies on third-party infrastructure for functions such as hosting, authentication, database storage, error monitoring and AI-assisted responses. Depending on the feature and deployment, providers may include Vercel, Supabase, Google or another configured AI provider, and Sentry. These providers process information to deliver or secure the service under their applicable terms and configurations.

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information where required by law, to respond to valid legal process, to protect users or the service, or to address abuse and security incidents. This policy is not a claim that the service has a security certification or independent audit.

5. Storage, offline use and synchronization

Some features use browser storage or device-local state to support preferences, sessions, offline work or retrying synchronization. Information saved locally may remain on that device until it is cleared or the app removes it. Clearing browser data or uninstalling the app may remove local copies, but it does not necessarily delete information already synchronized to our servers or retained by service providers.

6. Retention and deletion requests

Information is retained for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain account and learning history, resolve reliability or security issues, and meet applicable obligations. Different systems and providers may retain backups or logs for different periods. We do not currently publish a universal deletion deadline for every storage system.

You can request access to or deletion of account-associated information by emailing shadecodetech@gmail.com from your account email where possible. Include the request you want us to handle, but do not send your password. We may need to verify account ownership. Some information may remain temporarily in backups or be retained where required for security or legal reasons; we will explain material limitations when responding. A request by email is not an instant self-service deletion control.

7. Security

We use technical and organizational measures intended to limit access and protect information, including authenticated access controls and database permissions. No internet service can promise absolute security. Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately to shadecodetech@gmail.com.

8. Children and educational use

Shadecode Student is an educational product and may be used by school-age learners. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the request. Before use by younger children or through a school, a parent, guardian or institution should review this policy and the applicable service arrangements. We do not claim that this notice alone establishes compliance with every child-privacy or education-privacy law.

9. Your choices

  • Provide only the information needed for the feature you are using.
  • Review device/browser controls for cookies and local storage, understanding that changing them may affect sign-in or offline functionality.
  • Contact us with privacy, access or deletion questions.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the product and its providers change. The effective date above identifies the latest published version. Material changes should be reflected here before we rely on the changed practices.

Privacy contact: shadecodetech@gmail.com
