Shadecode Student · Account controls

Request account deletion

You can request deletion of your Shadecode Student account and the personal data associated with it, even if you have already uninstalled the app.

How to submit your request

  1. Send an email from the email address linked to your Shadecode Student account.
  2. Use the subject Shadecode Student account deletion request.
  3. Include your account email and, if known, your Shadecode username. Do not send your password.
  4. We will verify that you control the account, then process the deletion request and confirm the outcome by email.
Email a deletion request

If you cannot access your account email, contact us and explain the issue. We may ask for proportionate information to verify account ownership. Never send a password or one-time sign-in code.

What happens next

  • We verify the request to protect accounts from unauthorized deletion.
  • We delete or de-identify account-associated personal data in the systems we control, including requesting deletion from relevant service providers where applicable.
  • Some limited records may be retained where necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance or the resolution of disputes. Any retained information is restricted to that purpose.
  • Encrypted backups and operational logs may take additional time to expire from provider-managed retention cycles.

We will contact you if we need clarification and confirm when the request has been completed. Account deletion is permanent and may remove saved learning progress and other account-linked work.
