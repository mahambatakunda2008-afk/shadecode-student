## Practical and microscopy requirements

The SVG tissue-recognition guide is a schematic learning aid, not a photomicrograph or electron micrograph. Outcome 9.1.3 requires learners to recognise actual tissues in authentic microscope slides and micrographs. Outcome 9.1.4 requires recognition in those source types and accurate plan diagrams. These skills cannot be fully assessed by a schematic alone; authentic slide/micrograph examples and practical drawing exercises must be added to the learning experience before claiming complete practical readiness.
