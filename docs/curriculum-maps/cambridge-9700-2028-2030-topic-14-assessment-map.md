## Accuracy and scope notes

- Kidney content is limited to the named anatomical structures and the syllabus-specified processes of ultrafiltration and selective reabsorption in the PCT. Nephron diagrams are schematic, not authentic photomicrographs or electron micrographs.
- ADH increases collecting-duct water permeability by promoting aquaporin insertion; increased water reabsorption produces a smaller volume of more concentrated urine.
- The glucagon signalling sequence follows the syllabus order: receptor conformational change, G-protein, adenylyl cyclase, cAMP, protein kinase A, amplified phosphorylation cascade and glycogen breakdown in liver cells.
- Glucose oxidase generates hydrogen peroxide; peroxidase uses it to oxidise a chromogen in a colour test. Biosensor signals require calibration.
- Plant stomatal closure during water stress includes ABA and calcium ions as a second messenger. Guard-cell ion loss lowers turgor and closes the pore.
- Questions are original learning checks, not official Cambridge questions or mark schemes.
