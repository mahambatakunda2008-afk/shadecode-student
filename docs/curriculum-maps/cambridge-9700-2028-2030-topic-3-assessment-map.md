## Technical interpretation notes

- Initial rates are preferred for comparing enzyme activity because substrate depletion and product accumulation are initially limited.
- A colorimeter measures absorbance/transmission, not enzyme activity directly. A calibration relationship is needed when converting optical readings to concentration.
- At Km, the rate is one-half Vmax in the Michaelis–Menten model. A lower Km is commonly interpreted as higher apparent affinity under comparable conditions.
- For the simple models used to explain inhibition: competitive inhibition increases apparent Km while Vmax is unchanged; pure non-competitive inhibition decreases Vmax while Km is unchanged. Real inhibitors can show mixed behaviour, so the simplified patterns must be identified as model cases.
- Immobilisation may improve reuse and product separation, but diffusion limitations can reduce observed reaction rate.
