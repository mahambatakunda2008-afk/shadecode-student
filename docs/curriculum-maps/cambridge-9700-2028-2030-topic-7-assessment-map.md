## Assessment and editorial notes

- Both draft packages cover all 12 numbered Topic 7 outcomes.
- Questions are original learning checks with explanatory answers and indicative mark points, not Cambridge past-paper questions or official mark schemes.
- Original SVGs are explanatory schematics, not authentic microscope slides or photomicrographs. Learners must still practise interpreting real specimens and micrographs.
- Plan diagrams and annotated transverse sections require independent drawing-quality and accessibility review.
- All assets remain draft-only. Do not import, publish to learners or promote to the offline library until curriculum objective UUIDs are verified and independent Biology subject-editor, assessment and accessibility reviews pass.
