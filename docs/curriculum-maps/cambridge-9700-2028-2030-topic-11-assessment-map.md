## Scope notes

- Primary-response sequence includes macrophage antigen presentation, T-helper activation, B-cell clonal expansion and plasma-cell antibody secretion, and T-killer action.
- Antibody structure is linked to specificity, antigen binding and effector functions.
- Hybridoma coverage is an outline: immunisation, B-cell collection, fusion with myeloma cells, screening, cloning and culture.
- Immunity classifications are kept distinct: active/passive describes how antibodies or immune responses arise; natural/artificial describes whether medical intervention is involved.
- Vaccination programme coverage explains reduced susceptible hosts and indirect community protection. The graphic is a conceptual model, not a numerical threshold claim.
