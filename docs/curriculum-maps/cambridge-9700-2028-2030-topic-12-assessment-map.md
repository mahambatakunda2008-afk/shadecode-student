## Practical accuracy and limitations
- A CO₂-absorbing respirometer measures O₂ uptake, not RQ alone. RQ requires CO₂ output and O₂ uptake measured over the same interval.
- DCPIP and methylene blue decolourisation is an indirect indicator of electron transfer, not a direct ATP measurement.
- Diagrams are explanatory schematics, not authentic electron micrographs. Micrograph recognition and hands-on practical work remain necessary.
- No practical experiment was performed in this content commit.
- Independent subject-editor, assessment, accessibility and practical review plus objective UUID reconciliation are required before publication.
