## Known syllabus-boundary note

The water lesson restricts required Topic 2.4 coverage to solvent action, high specific heat capacity and latent heat of vaporisation. Cohesion, adhesion, surface tension, water as a metabolite and water as a habitat are not listed as required outcomes in Topic 2.4 of this syllabus and must be labelled as enrichment if included.

The Topic 2.2 asset explicitly includes the 2028–2030 outcome on reducing sugars (glucose, fructose and maltose) and non-reducing sucrose. This distinction was not adequately covered in the earlier chat-only explanation and is now included in the repository draft.
