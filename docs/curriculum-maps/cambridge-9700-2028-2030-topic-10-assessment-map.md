## Scope and accuracy notes

- The four malaria-causing species are listed individually as required: *Plasmodium falciparum*, *P. malariae*, *P. ovale* and *P. vivax*.
- The malaria graphic is a host-to-host vector transmission overview. It deliberately does not teach detailed parasite life-cycle stages, which the syllabus says are not expected for this topic.
- HIV transmission is described through specified infectious body fluids and routes. Casual contact and mosquito bites are explicitly distinguished from transmission routes.
- Penicillin is explained through inhibition of peptidoglycan cell-wall formation/cross-linking. Viruses lack this bacterial target.
- Resistance is explained through pre-existing variation or acquired resistance genes, selection, differential survival and reproduction, and increasing frequency. Antibiotics do not purposefully cause bacteria to adapt.
