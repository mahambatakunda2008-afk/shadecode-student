## Technical and scientific boundaries

- The water movement content uses water potential (Ψ) and does not introduce solute-potential or pressure-potential calculations.
- Water moves net from higher water potential to lower water potential across a partially permeable membrane.
- Visking tubing is explicitly presented as a model, not as a complete representation of a living cell surface membrane.
- Agar-block investigations are models of diffusion geometry; they do not reproduce all properties of living tissues.
- The zero percentage-mass-change point estimates the tissue water potential under the experimental conditions. It is not an exact value independent of temperature, equilibration, tissue variation or measurement error.
- All practicals require local risk assessment, teacher supervision, appropriate equipment and independent subject-editor review before classroom use.
- Questions and indicative marking points are original learning checks, not Cambridge past-paper questions or official mark schemes.
