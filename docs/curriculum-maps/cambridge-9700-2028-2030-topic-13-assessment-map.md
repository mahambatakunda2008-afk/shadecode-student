## Scientific and practical safeguards

- Rf is calculated as pigment distance divided by solvent-front distance, both measured from the baseline; reference values depend on solvent and conditions.
- DCPIP reduction is an indirect indicator of electron transfer in illuminated chloroplast suspensions. It is not a direct measurement of carbon fixation.
- Dark and boiled-chloroplast controls, comparable photon flux for wavelength comparisons, temperature control, equal chlorophyll concentration, repeats and objective absorbance readings are included in practical guidance.
- Whole-plant oxygen volume per unit time is preferred to bubble counts because bubble size varies. Light, CO2 availability, temperature and plant material must be controlled.
- The SVGs are explanatory schematics, not authentic electron micrographs or practical data. Learners still need practice interpreting real electron micrographs and carrying out practical work.
- The questions are original learning checks, not official Cambridge past-paper questions or mark schemes.
