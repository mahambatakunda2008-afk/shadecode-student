## Assessment emphasis

- Classification: compare species concepts, apply taxonomic ranks and distinguish domains using only specified features.
- Fieldwork: choose methods appropriate to organism mobility and the spatial question; justify random placement, replication and sample size.
- Quantitative ecology: practise Lincoln-index estimates, correlation interpretation and Simpson-index calculations using the formula convention supplied.
- Conservation: explain threat-to-decline causal pathways and evaluate the strengths and limitations of conservation strategies.
- Field activities must minimise disturbance, obtain permission and follow local safety guidance. Diagrams are explanatory schematics, not substitutes for field observation.

## Scientific guardrails

- Do not state that all Archaea are extremophiles or classify viruses as cellular members of the three domains.
- Distinguish habitat from niche, and species richness from evenness.
- Correlation does not establish causation.
- Simpson-index conventions differ. This package explains the common form D = 1 − Σ[n(n−1)]/[N(N−1)]; learners must use the formula and interpretation requested in the question.
- Evaluate Lincoln-index assumptions before treating an estimate as reliable.
- Ex situ breeding is not a substitute for protecting habitats and addressing causes of decline.
