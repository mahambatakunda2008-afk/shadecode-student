## Assessment emphasis

- Explain each tool by linking its molecular action to its role in the process.
- PCR: denaturation, primer annealing and extension; Taq is thermostable.
- Electrophoresis: DNA moves towards the positive electrode; smaller fragments generally travel further through the gel.
- Microarrays detect hybridisation to known probes; signal indicates complementary sequence and relative mRNA abundance under the method used.
- Medical applications require case-specific discussion of efficacy, risk, consent, privacy and equitable access.
- Agricultural examples must be distinguished: GM salmon growth, herbicide-tolerant soybean and Bt insect-resistant cotton.

## Scientific guardrails

- Fluorescent marker expression does not by itself prove that the target protein is functional.
- Gene editing can insert, delete or replace DNA; it does not necessarily transfer DNA from another species.
- Genetic screening often indicates risk or carrier status, not a guaranteed clinical outcome.
- Gene therapy outcomes depend on target cells, delivery, expression duration and immune responses.
- Resistance evolution and ecological effects should be discussed in the context of the specific trait and management system.
