## Scope and assessment notes

- The four expected mitosis stage names are prophase, metaphase, anaphase and telophase.
- Cytokinesis is treated separately from mitosis.
- The chromosome-count question distinguishes chromosome number from chromatid number after S phase.
- Original questions and indicative mark points are learning checks, not official Cambridge questions or mark schemes.
- Microscopy/practical activities require local risk assessment and teacher supervision.
- All packages remain draft. Database curriculum UUID/objective-key mapping and independent scientific, assessment and accessibility review are still required.
