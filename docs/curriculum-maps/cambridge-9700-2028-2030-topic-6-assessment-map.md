## Assessment and editorial notes

- All questions are original learning checks, not Cambridge past-paper questions or official mark schemes.
- Sequence questions explicitly distinguish coding and template strands and require correct 5′/3′ orientation.
- DNA polymerase synthesises in the 5′ to 3′ direction; DNA ligase joins Okazaki fragments.
- A frameshift is expected when insertion/deletion length is not a multiple of three.
