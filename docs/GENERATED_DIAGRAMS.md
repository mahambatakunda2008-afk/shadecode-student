# Generated diagrams

Diagrams are first-class generated content in both lessons and exams.

## Lessons

Cortex may generate diagrams when they materially improve understanding. Examples include force diagrams, graphs, geometry constructions, circuits, apparatus, biological structures and flowcharts.

A diagram can be required, recommended or optional. The generator must provide a purpose and accessible description.

## Exams

A diagram can be attached to a question when the assessment requires visual interpretation, construction or data extraction. The diagram must be part of the question specification and remain stable during marking.

## Representation

Prefer structured editable diagrams over raster images whenever possible. This allows zooming, accessibility descriptions, annotation, Canvas interaction and deterministic rendering.

## Quality requirements

Generated diagrams must be checked for:

- correct geometry;
- internally consistent labels;
- no missing referenced elements;
- no impossible or contradictory values;
- legibility at mobile sizes;
- accessibility description;
- correspondence with the question/lesson text.

A diagram that fails validation is repaired or regenerated before publication.

## Learner interaction

Generated diagrams can become Canvas objects so the learner can annotate, measure, redraw or answer directly on them without losing the original question/lesson state.
