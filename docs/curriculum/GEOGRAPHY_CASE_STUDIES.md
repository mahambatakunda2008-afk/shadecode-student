# Geography: Case Study Revision

Use the [O-Level Geography Case Study Revision Kit](../geo-case-templates/README.md) to organise the exact case studies assigned by your teacher.

Reusable resources:

- [Case study card](../geo-case-templates/CASE_STUDY_TEMPLATE.md): location, issue/process, causes, impacts, management, outcomes, evidence and source checks.
- [Retrieval flashcards](../geo-case-templates/FLASHCARD_TEMPLATE.md): short prompts for recall and exam application.

## Important curriculum boundary

This is a study method, not an official syllabus list and not a verified list of 24 required cases. The learner must enter the case-study titles from the teacher's instructions and confirm the relevant exam board, syllabus version and topic coverage before filling in the cards. Do not invent cases or claim the kit covers the whole syllabus.

## Suggested lesson format

Keep learning materials direct and scannable:

1. **Notes:** short explanations organised by heading.
2. **Definitions:** precise terms with a simple meaning.
3. **Examples:** worked geographical examples with verified place-specific evidence.
4. **Recall:** flashcards and closed-book questions.
5. **Exam practice:** a syllabus-appropriate question, marking guidance and targeted feedback.

Avoid long fictional narratives where the task is factual revision. If a story or scenario is used for a specific teaching purpose, label it clearly and do not present it as real case-study evidence.
