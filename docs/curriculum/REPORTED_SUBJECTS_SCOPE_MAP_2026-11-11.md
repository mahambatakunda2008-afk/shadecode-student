# Reported subject scope map: initial official-source extraction

Date: 2026-11-11

## Trust boundary

This is an initial, source-cited scope map for the four subjects reported missing by learners. It records qualification boundaries, top-level topic architecture and paper structure visible in the awarding body's current public documents. It is **not** a complete normalized syllabus pack, a lesson library, an assessment bank, or a claim of full curriculum verification.

Before a syllabus can move to verified/production-ready, Shadecode must retrieve and fingerprint the exact source PDF and every update notice, extract every numbered topic/subtopic and learning outcome, assessment objective and weighting, command word, component rule, practical/fieldwork requirement where relevant, and complete the repository's 25 syllabus coverage dimensions with independent review. Preserve exam-year editions separately.

## Cambridge International AS & A Level Business 9609

- Source edition: 2026, 2027 and 2028; Current document: Version 2, published December 2025. The 2026–2028 syllabus update is a separate one-page PDF; the syllabus states there are no significant changes affecting teaching.
- Official syllabus PDF: https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/697371-2026-2028-syllabus.pdf
- Official subject page: https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-international-as-and-a-level-business-9609/\n- Official 2026–2028 update PDF: https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/748947-2026-2028-syllabus-update.pdf
- AS scope: topics 1.1–5.5.
- A Level scope: topics 1.1–10.4. AS content is assumed knowledge for Papers 3 and 4.
- AS topic families: Business and its environment; Human resource management; Marketing; Operations management; Finance and accounting.
- A Level extends these through topics 6–10 in the same five families.
- Assessment: Paper 1 Business Concepts 1; Paper 2 Business Concepts 2; Paper 3 Business Decision-Making; Paper 4 Business Strategy.
- Teaching design: preserve application, analysis, evaluation and justified recommendation skills. Case-study decision-making and strategy cannot be reduced to definitions.
- Source locators: syllabus overview pp. 8–12; subject content pp. 13–36; assessment details pp. 37–39.

## Cambridge International AS & A Level Economics 9708

- Source edition: 2026, 2027 and 2028; Version 1, published September 2023.
- Official syllabus PDF: https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/697423-2026-2028-syllabus.pdf
- Official subject page: https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-international-as-and-a-level-economics-9708/\n- Official 2026–2028 update PDF: https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/748950-2026-2028-syllabus-update.pdf
- AS scope: topics 1.1–6.5.
- A Level scope: topics 1.1–11.6. AS content is assumed knowledge for Papers 3 and 4.
- AS topic families: Basic economic ideas and resource allocation; The price system and the microeconomy; Government microeconomic intervention; The macroeconomy; Government macroeconomic intervention; International economic issues.
- A Level topics 7–11 extend the price system/microeconomy, government microeconomic intervention, macroeconomy, government macroeconomic intervention and economic development.
- Assessment: Paper 1 AS Multiple Choice; Paper 2 AS Data Response and Essays; Paper 3 A Level Multiple Choice; Paper 4 A Level Data Response and Essays.
- Teaching design: diagrams, numerical work, data response, economic analysis and evaluation are core capabilities. Lessons and assessments must support graph interpretation and construction, not text-only answers.
- Source locators: syllabus overview pp. 8–14; subject content pp. 15–34; assessment details pp. 35–37.

## Cambridge International AS & A Level Geography 9696

- Source edition: 2027, 2028 and 2029; Version 1, published September 2024.
- Official syllabus PDF: https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/718332-2027-2029-syllabus.pdf
- Official subject page: https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-international-as-and-a-level-geography-9696/
- AS candidates study all six compulsory topics:
  - Paper 1 Physical Geography: Hydrology, river processes and hazards; Atmospheric processes and global climate change; Earth processes and mass movements.
  - Paper 2 Human Geography: Population and migration; Water resources and management; Urban areas and management.
- A Level candidates study all AS content plus four optional topics, two from each paper:
  - Paper 3 Global Environments: Tropical environments; Coastal environments; Hazardous environments; Arid environments.
  - Paper 4 Global Themes: Climate change impacts and governance; Environmental issues and management; Trade, aid and tourism; Disease and geography.
- Assessment: Papers 1–4, each 1 hour 30 minutes and 60 marks. Papers 1 and 2 are compulsory at AS; A Level candidates take all four. Papers 3 and 4 require a choice of two topics from four.
- Teaching design: selected A Level options must drive topic visibility, lesson plans and exam practice. Preserve map/diagram/resource interpretation, data analysis, case-specific evidence and essay skills. The 2027–2029 edition changes topic content and assessment objectives from the 2025–2026 edition; do not merge editions.
- Source locators: content overview pp. 8–9; detailed subject content pp. 14–48; assessment details pp. 49–52; changes pp. 58–59.
