# Product Research Input 01

**Source:** User-provided copy of a shared ChatGPT research conversation
**Date incorporated:** 2026-08-08
**Authority:** Research input, not an approved product decision

## Core findings

1. Shadecode Student currently has a promising concept/prototype but has not yet become a genuinely strong student product.
2. The largest strategic concern is insufficiently sharp product differentiation. The product must give students a compelling reason to choose it over existing tools such as ChatGPT, YouTube, school portals, messaging, storage, and past-paper resources.
3. There is a risk of becoming a collection of mediocre features. Product development should prioritize excellent end-to-end student workflows over feature count.
4. The core experience should make the student's next useful action obvious immediately after opening the product.
5. Educational content quality is a trust-critical dependency: explanations, questions, answers, difficulty, curriculum alignment, and assessment coverage must be reliable.
6. Product-market evidence is currently insufficient. Repeated voluntary usage, return behavior, and referrals are stronger evidence than positive verbal reactions.
7. The long-term opportunity is broader than an LMS, potentially integrating curriculum, learning, practice, assessment, progress, and feedback into a coherent student workflow.
8. The research warns against building the entire long-term ecosystem before proving a core student workflow.

## Product implications

### Dashboard

The dashboard should answer three questions quickly:

- Where am I?
- What matters right now?
- What should I do next?

It should not become a feature directory.

### Product strategy

Prioritize depth and quality in core student workflows before expanding breadth. New features should be evaluated against whether they materially improve learning outcomes, reduce friction, or strengthen the core loop.

### Trust

Do not fabricate personalization, predictions, grades, mastery, or recommendations. If the system lacks evidence, it should communicate uncertainty or omit the claim.

### Measurement

Usage instrumentation should eventually distinguish discovery from genuine product value: activation, completed learning actions, return usage, study-plan adherence, assessment completion, and voluntary re-engagement.
