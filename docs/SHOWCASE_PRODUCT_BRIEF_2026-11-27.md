# Shadecode Student — Event Brief

**Prepared for:** Speech and Prize Giving Day, 27 November 2026  
**Audience:** Students, parents, educators, school leadership and invited public officials  
**Product being demonstrated:** Shadecode Student  
**Public entry point at preparation time:** https://shadecodestudent.vercel.app/

## What Shadecode Student is

Shadecode Student is a personal learning system designed to bring a learner's study activities into one connected experience. Instead of treating lessons, questions, mistakes, revision, exams, projects and progress as disconnected activities, the product is being built to turn learning activity into useful evidence that can help a learner decide what to do next.

Its central learning loop is:

**Observe → Understand → Predict → Act → Evaluate → Learn**

Cortex is the learning-intelligence foundation behind this direction. Models can help explain or generate learning material, but deterministic learning state, marking and saved progress must not depend on a model being available.

## Product areas described in the current repository

- Learn: curriculum-aware, AI-assisted learning workflows.
- Exam Simulation and Exam Hub: assessment, timed practice and past-paper/question-bank workflows.
- Cortex: learning signals, weak-area foundations and recommendations.
- Study organisation: tasks, timetables, focus and analytics.
- Project Studio: staged project planning and evidence capture.
- Cortex Verify: tools for capturing and reviewing written work.
- Gamification: XP, levels, achievements, streaks, challenges and leaderboards.
- PWA/local-first foundations: a continuing effort to make supported learning work resilient to unreliable connectivity.

**Important maturity note:** The repository explicitly says feature maturity varies. Not every area should be presented as complete. The live demonstration must use only flows that have been tested immediately before the event. Offline capability must be described per tested workflow, not as a blanket claim.

## Why this matters

Learners do not all struggle with the same concepts, learn at the same pace, or have consistent access to connectivity. A useful learning system should help learners practise, understand mistakes, preserve their work and make a sensible next step.

Shadecode is being developed in Zimbabwe, with a focus on making capable learning technology relevant to local learners and their real study conditions. It is intended to expand beyond secondary exam preparation over time, but this presentation demonstrates Shadecode Student as it exists today.

## What we are asking from the audience

We are not asking the audience to assume that the product is finished or that it has already been adopted by institutions. We welcome:
- students willing to try the product and give honest feedback;
- teachers and school leaders interested in exploring a carefully scoped pilot;
- education stakeholders willing to discuss local learning needs and responsible technology adoption;
- practical feedback about curriculum fit, connectivity, accessibility and classroom use.

A pilot should begin with a small group, agreed learning activities, clear consent and privacy boundaries, and measurable feedback. It should not promise improved grades before evidence exists.

## Questions we are ready to answer

**Does it replace teachers?** No. The product is intended to support students' learning and help them practise and reflect. It does not replace teachers or school judgement.

**Does it work without internet?** Some model-free and local-first foundations exist, but offline support is not universal. We will demonstrate only the exact offline workflow that has passed an airplane-mode test.

**Is all the AI always available?** No. External model services can fail or be unavailable. The architecture is designed to provide deterministic and local fallbacks where supported, and to fail honestly where they are not.

**Is it only for Cambridge?** Cambridge and ZIMSEC learners are an important focus, while broader levels and tertiary study are part of the platform's direction. We will state the exact subject and syllabus coverage verified for the demonstrated workflow.

**Has the school or government endorsed it?** No endorsement should be implied. The event is an opportunity to demonstrate the product and invite discussion.
