## Likely questions and safe answers

**Will this replace teachers?** No. It is intended to support students' learning, not replace teachers or professional judgement.

**Can it work offline?** Some selected workflows are being made local-first. We only claim offline support for the exact activities tested on the demonstrated device.

**What if the AI is unavailable?** AI providers can fail. The product is designed to use deterministic or local alternatives where available and to report honestly when a capability requires a provider.

**Does it cover every subject and syllabus?** No universal claim should be made. We can show the exact subject, level and syllabus content verified in the current product.

**Can our school use it?** We would be interested in discussing a small, consent-based pilot, first agreeing on learner group, goals, support, data boundaries and evaluation.

**Has it improved grades?** We are not claiming measured grade improvements without a proper evaluation. We want to test usefulness and outcomes responsibly.

**Who built it?** Shadecode is developing the product. Describe the actual team and roles accurately; do not imply a larger team or institutional backing than exists.
