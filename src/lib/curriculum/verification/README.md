# Production curriculum verification

Curriculum-specific teaching and assessment claims must be grounded in an exact, authoritative syllabus version whose whole-syllabus coverage has been reconciled.

Verification is broader than objectives. A production bundle must account for the syllabus identity, source document, structure, scope/content, competencies and skills, progression, assessment objectives and structure, paper components and weightings, examination format, practical/project/coursework requirements where applicable, terminology, constraints, guidance, resources, and assessment evidence such as past papers, mark schemes, examiner reports and grade thresholds where applicable.

A dimension may be explicitly marked `not_applicable` only when the official source establishes that the dimension does not apply to that curriculum/version. `draft` or `discovered` evidence never counts as production verification.

## Cambridge IGCSE Computer Science 0478 (2026-2028)

The official syllabus is version 5, 56 pages, and identifies ten content topics. The authoritative source is the Cambridge International syllabus PDF recorded in the curriculum source registry.

The ingestion pipeline must preserve provenance, document hashes, and source/version evidence. It must never auto-promote incomplete or merely discovered material to verified production knowledge.
